Sophia · Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

In short

Sophia: My AI Girlfriend helps you chat with and create AI companions. We use information needed to provide the app, keep it working, and improve your experience. We do not sell your personal information.

Information you provide

This may include your account details, messages, companion preferences, feedback, and images you choose to upload or create. Chat messages and image requests are sent to our service to provide responses and image features. Our service uses OpenAI technology to process chat and image requests; those requests may include the text or images you submit. Please avoid sharing information you would not want processed for these features.

How we use information

  • To provide chats, companion creation, and image features.
  • To maintain your account, chat history, preferences, and app features.
  • To understand app usage and improve reliability through analytics.
  • To deliver notifications if you allow them, and respond to support or feedback requests.

Permissions and your choices

Notifications are optional. If you save an image, the app may ask for permission to add it to your photo library. You can choose a reference image through the device’s image picker. You can manage permissions in your device settings. You can also clear chat history or request account help by contacting us.

Service providers and storage

We use service providers to operate the app, including providers for AI processing, account and app services, analytics, notifications, and advertising. They process information as needed to provide their services. Information may be processed in countries outside your own. We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app and meet legal or operational needs; retention can vary by service.

Analytics and diagnostics

Firebase Analytics records app screen views and app events to help us understand usage and improve the app. These events may include screen names and related app context or event details; this app code does not intentionally add chat message text to the analytics events described here. Firebase Crashlytics is configured to collect fatal Flutter errors in release mode, helping us diagnose crashes. Analytics and diagnostics are provided by Google and may process device or app information as described in Google’s privacy terms.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads, including banner and full-screen formats. Google’s consent tools may request consent information and show a consent form where required; ad requests are made according to the consent status available to the app. Ad providers may process device identifiers and app or ad interaction information to deliver, measure, and help prevent fraud in advertising, subject to your choices and applicable settings.

In-app purchases

Sophia offers subscriptions or other digital purchases through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. The store processes payment details; we do not receive your full payment card number. The app receives purchase status and product information to activate or restore access. Purchase verification information is sent to our service to verify entitlement. Store billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are also subject to the relevant store’s terms and settings.

Important: Apple Standard EULA

For users who download Sophia from Apple’s App Store, use of the app is subject to Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement (EULA), in addition to our Terms of Service. This privacy policy explains how information is handled; it does not replace the EULA or other applicable Apple agreements.

Terms of Service

Your use of Sophia is also subject to our Terms of Service and, where applicable, the store terms for purchases.

Your choices and contact

You can stop using the app at any time and control device permissions in Settings. To ask a privacy question, request access or deletion of account information, or contact us about this policy, email support@developerinfotech.com.
